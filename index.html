Rainforest Retreat • Kukuleganga, Ayagama

Escape into the raptures of the fauna, flora and the flowing streams.

Escape into the raptures of the fauna, flora and the flowing streams. A landscape embedded with rare trees and endemic medicinal plants, waterfalls and streams.

'Rainforest Retreat' is neatly tucked away amidst the rain forest terrain of the Kukuleganga region, an area famous for its many waterways. Enjoy a peaceful retreat amidst the looming trees, surrounded by the music of the humming birds and the babbling brook.

Many guests have mentioned the rare energy felt within our premises. "Easier to focus quickly and to meditate, healing energy, ability to get into a stance more easily" are some of the comments.

Protecting this valuable energy and the gifts of nature, in a sustainable manner, is our prime objective.

'Supporting the village community for sustainable development' is another objective and a strategy.

The location facility and layout of the Rainforest Retreat provides an ideal place to get away from the hustle and bustle of daily life enabling you to mingle with the simple pleasures of nature.

          How to Reach Rainforest Retreat
          
    Access Routes
    
          
    Kukuleganga, Ayagama
    
        
    
        
      
    

      
      
    
        
    
          Route 01 • Southern Expressway
          
    ● From Southern Express way Dodangoda Exit. Proceed to Matugama and along the Matugama Kelinkanda Road, upto the ‘Kukule Electricity Distribution Centre’.
    
        
    

        
    
          Route 02 • From Colombo
          
    ● Colombo, Ingiriya, Egaloya, Bulathsinhala, Molkawa and to the left upto ‘Kukule Electricity Distribution Centre’.
    
        
    

        
    
          Route 03 • From Ratnapura
          
    ● Ratnapura- Along Panadura Road, turn off to Egal oya Road, 1k. m. before Ingiriya town. To Egaloya, Bulathsinhala, Molkawa.
    
        
    

        
        
    
          ⚠️ Final 2 km Access Notice
          
    
            The final 2 km leading to the retreat can ONLY be accessed by a 4-Wheel Drive (4WD) vehicle or a Three-Wheeler (Tuk-Tuk).
